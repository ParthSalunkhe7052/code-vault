# High-End "Abstract Industrialism" Image Prompts

These prompts are designed for 2025 SaaS meta (Linear/Stripe style). They focus on **Physicality, Depth, and Infrastructure**.

## Theme: Clean Infrastructure
**Materials:** Frosted Glass, Matte Gunmetal, White Ceramic, Internal Glowing Cores.
**Lighting:** Cinematic Studio Lighting, Soft Rim Lights (Indigo/Emerald), Dark Ambient Void.

---

### 1. Hero Visual ("The Core")
> Photorealistic 3D product shot of an abstract "Security Core". A large, heavy translucent frosted glass monolith floating in a dark ambient void. Inside the glass are glowing, interconnected fiber-optic neural pathways in indigo and emerald. Soft studio lighting with realistic depth of field. 8k, raytracing, minimalist, elegant, expensive feel.

### 2. Step 1: Drop Script ("The Entry")
> High-fidelity 3D render of a minimalist metal slot or "entry port" suspended in space. A translucent digital document (glass card) with a floating Code icon is hovering near the slot. Abstract data streams are flowing into the port. Matte gunmetal and frosted glass textures. Soft indigo glow. Sharp focus, clean background.

### 3. Step 2: Compilation ("The Forge")
> 3D abstract visualization of a "Digital Forge". A stream of chaotic, floating glass shards (representing raw code) being compressed and fused into a single, perfectly smooth obsidian-black cube by glowing magnetic fields. Cinematic lighting, high contrast, industrial-premium aesthetic.

### 4. Step 3: Hardware Locking ("The Anchor")
> A photorealistic 3D schematic of a futuristic microchip. The center of the chip features a glowing, deeply embedded physical "vault lock" made of white ceramic and gold. The chip is mounted on a matte black floating substrate. Soft rim lighting, ultra-detailed textures, 8k resolution.

### 5. Step 4: Distribution/Profit ("The Mint")
> A stack of premium, semi-transparent "License Cards" floating in a tiered arrangement. Each card features glowing holographic engravings and a small success badge. In the far background, a soft, out-of-focus rising line chart made of light. Elegant, high-end finance/SaaS aesthetic.
